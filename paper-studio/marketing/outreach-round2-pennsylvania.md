# Arguo outreach emails: Pennsylvania (round 2)

## 1. University of Pennsylvania · Graduate Student Center (Philadelphia)

To: gradcenter@upenn.edu  
Source: https://gsc.upenn.edu/contact  
Subject: A free paper-checking tool for Penn graduate writers (feedback welcome)

```
Dear Graduate Student Center team,

I'm Steven Niu, and I'm building Arguo, a free tool that helps researchers check their papers without writing any of the text for them. I'm reaching out to universities and research institutes in Pennsylvania first. Since the Center supports graduate and professional students through thesis and dissertation writing, I'd value your view on whether this could help them.

What it does:
- Citation Checker, to catch references that don't exist or have wrong details: https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev/citation-checker/
- Reference Formatter, for more than 10,000 journal styles: https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev/reference-formatter/
- Research Gap Finder, which lists recent papers on a topic with a briefing that cites only those papers: https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev/gap-finder/
The full workspace also checks a draft section by section and explains how to revise, without ever writing text.

It is in early testing and free, and it deliberately never generates text, so writers have nothing to disclose to journals.

If you have ten minutes, I'd be grateful for your honest opinion. I'd also be glad to show it in a 15-minute call or in person, if that is easier. If it isn't relevant, there's no need to reply, and I won't follow up.

Thank you for your time,
Steven Niu
Creator of Arguo
https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev
```
