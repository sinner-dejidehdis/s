# Arguo outreach emails (round 1)

## 1. Brandeis University (Writing centre)

To: writingcenter@brandeis.edu  
Source: https://www.brandeis.edu/writing-center/graduate-writing-support.html  
Subject: A free reference-checking tool for graduate writers (feedback welcome)

```
Dear Brandeis Writing Center team,

I'm Steven Niu, and I'm building Arguo, a free tool that helps researchers check their papers without writing any of the text for them. Your graduate writing support works with exactly the writers it is meant to help, so your view would be worth more to me than anyone's.

What it does:
- Checks every reference against Crossref and flags ones that don't exist or contain errors, which helps when AI chatbots have invented citations: https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev/citation-checker/
- Compares a draft's research question, methods, results and discussion section by section, and explains how to revise with examples, instead of rewriting.
- Formats reference lists in the style of more than 10,000 journals.

It is in early testing and free. It deliberately never generates text, so writers have nothing to disclose.

If you have ten minutes, I'd be grateful for your honest opinion: is it useful, is anything misleading, and would you ever point a writer to it? If it isn't relevant, there's no need to reply, and I won't follow up.

Thank you for your time,
Steven Niu
Creator of Arguo
https://arguolucid.arguolucid.workers.dev
```
