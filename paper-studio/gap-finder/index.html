Questions

Can the briefing invent papers?

No. The AI only sees the papers listed on this page, and every point must cite them by number. Points without a valid citation are removed.

Where do the papers come from?

From OpenAlex, an open index of more than 250 million scholarly works.

Is the briefing a literature review I can use?

No. It is a map to help you decide what to read. Read the papers and write the review in your own words.

Is there a limit?

The AI briefing counts toward the free daily AI checks. The paper search itself has no limit.
