# Gaming Wiki

Hi there, again, this is a gaming wiki.

Let's start with common games types. Not a lot of them I have played, but the most common one would be single player games. These games are restricted to only one player at a time. They often combine a range of genres, i.e. advanture and history. They often are a consecutive story requiring a lengthy amount of time and effort to complete the major quest, let alone minor advantures that might came up during your explorations. 

The second leading type is probably PVP games. Compared to the previous type, this type of game usually has a larger number of player at a time ranging from two to 100+. The playability of this kind is incredably huge due to the infinant possibility of human.
